MAGPOW Brand

Built for Serious Work.

MAGPOW is River Trading Ltd's tools and hardware brand, developed specifically for Rwanda's growing community of builders, craftsmen, electricians, and technical professionals who need tools that perform reliably on the job, every day.

The name MAGPOW reflects the brand's positioning — powerful, magnetic reliability for professional work. Every MAGPOW product is selected and tested for durability under real working conditions in Rwanda's construction and maintenance environment.

The MAGPOW range covers hand tools, measuring equipment, fastening tools, electrical accessories, and construction hardware — giving tradespeople access to a trusted brand they can depend on across different trades.
