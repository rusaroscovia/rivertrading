What We Do

Wholesale & Retail Distribution

We supply a broad catalogue of industrial, hardware and household goods to individual buyers, contractors, and small-to-large hardware stores across Rwanda and the East African region.

Product supply categories

  • Construction & hardwareWall plaster, cement, adhesives, contact glue, silicone sealant, thinner, welding electrodes and discs, helmets, raincoats, measuring tapes, and building materials.
  • Electrical suppliesElectric cables and wires, circuit breakers, switches, sockets, metal and plastic junction boxes, and lighting equipment.
  • Plumbing & sanitary itemsPipes, fittings, and bathroom and sanitary fixtures.
  • Chemicals & industrial oilsCaustic soda, machine lubricants, anti-rust oils, and motor oils.
  • Hand tools, power tools & equipmentPliers, wire cutters, glass cutters, carpenter’s planers, pneumatic hammers, welding machines, and padlocks.
  • Hygiene & paper productsToilet paper, paper towels, and cleaning agents.
⇄ Import & Export Photo Add image: images/services-import-export.jpg

Import & Export Operations

We source and import products for Rwanda through international partners, supporting the supply of construction, industrial, and household goods. Our trading activity also connects buyers and sellers across borders.

Products and sourcing markets

  • Construction, electrical, hand and power tools, plumbing and sanitary products.
  • Hygienic tissues, agricultural tools and machines, and industrial raw materials such as chemicals.
  • International sourcing partners in China (PRC), India, Italy, Kenya, Egypt, Tanzania, and Dubai (UAE).
⚙ Manufacturing Photo Add image: images/services-manufacturing.jpg

Local Manufacturing & Production

We produce selected cleaning, hygiene and car-care products under our proprietary RIVER® and MIRAH® brands for customers in Rwanda and the region.

Products include

  • Liquid detergents and laundry liquids.
  • Hand wash and shampoos for personal and animal care.
  • Acetone-based nail polish removers, glass cleaners, and machine lubricants.
  • Wheel bins and sanitation accessories.
⌖ Shopping & Delivery Photo Add image: images/services-shopping-delivery.jpg

In-Store Shopping, Pick-Up & Delivery

Customers can shop directly at our Kigali and provincial locations, collect orders in person, or request delivery. We serve both commercial and residential customers.

  • Direct counter service at our Kigali and provincial locations.
  • Order pick-up and delivery options for residential and commercial customers.

B2B & Project Supply

We support business customers and building projects with planned supply of project-grade materials and hardware in the quantities their work requires.

  • Formal quotations for business and project requirements.
  • Project-grade material fulfilment for building and construction sites.
  • Bulk hardware provisions for small and large projects.
