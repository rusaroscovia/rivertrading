Four Brands, One Standard

From our founding, River Trading Ltd has done more than import and distribute quality products — we build brands of our own. R RIVER® 01 — The Flagship Brand ▼

RIVER® is the original brand of River Trading Ltd and the most recognised name in our range. Born from our founding commitment to bring quality products to Rwanda at fair prices, it now covers lubricants, cleaning products, adhesives, hygiene items and everyday household essentials.

Every RIVER® product is manufactured or sourced to meet strict quality standards before it reaches a customer. When you see the RIVER® name, you know exactly what to expect — quality you can trust, every time.

M Mirah 02 — Personal Care Brand ▼

Mirah is our personal care brand — gentle, effective hygiene and beauty products made for Rwandan homes, salons, schools, hotels and healthcare facilities. It was created to give consumers locally available products that are consistent in quality and affordable in daily use.

The range covers shampoos for men, women and children, hand wash, body lotion, conditioner and skin care basics — everyday care, done properly.

D Diamond Mix 03 — Cleaning Solutions Brand ▼

Diamond Mix is our surface and floor cleaning brand — powerful concentrates formulated for hard floors, tiles, kitchen counters and commercial spaces, delivering a streak-free clean with a long-lasting fresh scent.

The concentrate formula goes further: a small amount diluted with water covers a large area, making it cost-efficient for homes, offices, schools, clinics and hotels. Available in Classic and Lemon Fresh.

M MAGPOW 04 — Tools & Hardware Brand ▼

MAGPOW is our professional tools and hardware brand, developed for Rwanda's builders, craftsmen, electricians and technical professionals. Every product is selected and tested for durability under real working conditions in construction and maintenance work.

The range covers hand tools, measuring equipment, fastening tools, electrical accessories and construction hardware — a trusted brand across different trades.
