RIVER® Brand

Quality & Good Service — Made by River Trading Ltd

RIVER® is the original and most recognized brand of River Trading Ltd. Born from our founding commitment to bring quality products to Rwanda at fair prices, the RIVER® brand now covers a wide range of products used daily in homes, garages, construction sites, and businesses across the country.

Every RIVER® product is manufactured or sourced to meet strict quality standards before it reaches a customer. The brand stands for reliability — when you see the RIVER® name, you know what to expect.

From our bestselling RIVER® Machine Lubricant to RIVER® Car Wash Soap, RIVER® Contact Adhesive, and RIVER® Nail Polish Remover — each product carries the same promise: quality you can trust, every time.
