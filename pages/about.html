WHO WE ARE

Quality Products,
Trusted Across Rwanda.

River Trading Ltd is a Kigali-based import and export company with over 12 years of experience. We supply a wide range of products for construction, electrical work, plumbing, wall plastering, and construction chemicals, as well as food ingredients and cosmetics.

Alongside our popular RIVER® brand, we have expanded into hygiene products. We manufacture liquid detergent, laundry liquid, hand wash, and shampoos for people and animals. We also import toilet paper and towels.

We also import a wide range of products, mainly from China (PRC), Kenya, Egypt, Tanzania, India, and Dubai in the United Arab Emirates, serving customers from individuals to small and large hardware businesses.

We have built a strong reputation in Rwanda’s construction sector by combining our experience with high-quality products and responsive customer care.

Our highly qualified staff are delighted to welcome and assist you at our main office, branches, and through our agents across the country.

Contact us for further information, support, or delivery assistance.

Mission & Vision

Our purpose is to serve Rwanda with quality, integrity, and a lasting commitment to the communities we support.

Our Mission

To build on our beginnings as a small hardware shop founded in 2010 and serve customers across Rwanda and the region with trusted products and dependable service.

  1. Market leadershipBuild on our beginnings to become a leading regional distributor and manufacturer.
  2. Quality and trustDeliver reliable, high-standard building, electrical, plumbing, chemical, and hygiene products, including our RIVER and MIRAH brands.
  3. Customer-centred growthProvide dependable service to individual customers and large hardware businesses throughout Rwanda and the wider region.

Our Vision

To be among the region’s most trusted providers of quality construction, industrial, and household products, serving customers across Rwanda and East Africa.

  1. Regional trustEarn lasting trust by consistently providing quality products and dependable service throughout Rwanda and East Africa.
  2. Wider accessStrengthen our distribution so more homes, businesses, and industries can access the products they need.
  3. Trusted brandsBuild RIVER and MIRAH into recognized brands associated with quality, reliability, and value.
  4. Lasting partnershipsDevelop strong, long-term relationships with customers, suppliers, and business partners.
  5. Shared progressGrow responsibly while contributing to the success of the communities and markets we serve.
