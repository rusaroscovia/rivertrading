OUR STORY

Quality Products,
Trusted Across Rwanda.

River Trading Ltd is a company specializing in import and export in Kigali, Rwanda. With more than 10 years of experience in Construction, Electric, Plumbing, Wall Plaster, and Chemical materials used in construction, Food ingredients, and Cosmetics.

Although our RIVER® brand is very popular in the Rwandan market, we have also added hygiene-related products. We now manufacture all kinds of hygienic products such as Liquid Detergent, Laundry Liquid, Hand Wash, and all kinds of Shampoo — for men and animals — without forgetting the importation of toilet paper and towels of all kinds.

We have also various other products that are imported mostly from PRC, Kenya, Egypt, Tanzania, India, and Dubai City in the UAE — as a solution to our esteemed customers ranging from individuals to small and big hardware businesses.

Our reputation is significant in the Rwanda building sector for combining experience with high-quality products and responsive customer care.

Our highly qualified staff are always delighted to welcome you at our main offices, our branches, and our agents countrywide.

Keep contacting us for further information, support, or delivery.

Mission & Vision

Everything we do is guided by a clear purpose — to serve Rwanda with quality, honesty, and long-term commitment.

🎯

Our Mission

To supply Rwandan homes, businesses, and industries with high-quality, affordable products — delivered with exceptional service, speed, and care.

  • Ensure every client receives consistent, verified product quality
  • Offer fair and competitive pricing for all
  • Build partnerships based on trust and transparency
  • Respond quickly with efficient delivery nationwide
  • Support Rwanda's economic growth through local commerce
🔭

Our Vision

To become the most trusted trading company in Rwanda and across East Africa — setting the standard for product quality, service excellence, and reliability.

  • Expand our product range to cover every industry and home
  • Establish distribution across all provinces of Rwanda
  • Grow RIVER® as a recognized mark of quality
  • Enter East African markets within the next 5 years
  • Contribute to Rwanda's Vision 2050
