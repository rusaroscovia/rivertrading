Who We Are

Rwanda's Go-To Partner for
Quality Products

"Quality and Good Service" — it's not just a tagline, it's our commitment to every customer.

River Trading Ltd is a Rwandan-based company specializing in the supply of high-quality industrial, household, and commercial products. From premium lubricants and cleaning solutions to professional tools and hardware, we bring the best to your doorstep.

We are dedicated to empowering businesses, craftsmen, and homeowners across Rwanda with reliable products that stand the test of time. Our team works tirelessly to source, distribute, and deliver products you can trust.
